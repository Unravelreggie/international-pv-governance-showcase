# Governance and evidence methods

The inspected operational project covers country-product approval scopes, registration states, agreement governance and responsibility matrices, versioned document association, service-provider master data and follow-up tasks. Protected server authorization and auditability are separate from UI filters.

This independent demonstration uses four invented scopes with fictional product, region, role and document labels. It distinguishes signed, draft, unknown and expired agreement states. A file label alone does not confirm signature status; a signed flag without its evidence triggers review. Unknown does not mean confirmed absent. Display filtering is not RBAC, and a browser acknowledgement is not a durable audit trail.

The toy follow-up date subtracts three calendar months from a fictional first-sale date with end-of-month clamping. This is an illustrative scheduling policy only, not a stated legal requirement or a substitute for the actual approved agreement's deadlines. A fixed as-of date keeps the demonstration reproducible. No real license, agreement, responsibility assignment, invoice, sales figure or regulatory text is included.

The UI deliberately reports readiness for human review rather than legal compliance or authorization to sell. Actual record amendments, access decisions, contractual review and regulated exports are outside the demo.
